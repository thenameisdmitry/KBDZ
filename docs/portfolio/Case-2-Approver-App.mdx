# Case 2: Approver Application

**Format:** A step-by-step instructional guide covering the full setup and operation of the Approver mobile application. It enables users to review, approve, or reject transactions on the go.

**Description:** The article walks users through initial configuration, authentication, and day-to-day operations. It is a critical routine that ensures that time-sensitive Transaction requests are verified immediately.

**Business impact:** The mobile app patches a critical operational gap in Transactions management workflow: the ability to approve or reject transactions on the go. This is critical when managing asset movements under credit or collateral agreements, where delays can carry significant financial and contractual risk.

**Target readers:** All users of the Transaction Manager module, including treasury operations managers, collateral management officers, fund controllers, and credit operations specialists.
