# Retrieval Pipeline, Stage by Stage

The [assistant](./docs-assistant) shows what retrieval does. This shows where it breaks,
and who fixes each break.

<RetrievalPipeline />

## Why the ownership labels matter

When an agent answers wrongly, the reflex is to treat it as a model problem: swap the
embedding model, raise the top-k, widen the context window.

Five of the seven stages fail for reasons that live in the documentation:

| Stage | The writer's share |
|---|---|
| Query | Headings use internal vocabulary instead of the customer's |
| Chunk | A fact depends on the paragraph above it and does not survive the split |
| Retrieve | The answer exists but shares no wording with the question |
| Generate | The chunks do not contain the answer, so the model composes one |
| Cite | The cited section does not support the claim |

Only embedding and reranking are genuinely infrastructure. No amount of tuning recovers a
fact that was never written in a findable place.

## The stage worth staring at

**Generate.** A model handed three passages that do not answer the question almost never
says so. It produces a fluent paragraph in the register of your documentation, with your
terminology, and the customer cannot tell it apart from a real answer.

The dangerous output is not the blank one. It is the confident one, which is why
[RAG Evaluation](./rag-evaluation) measures answers rather than trusting prose.
